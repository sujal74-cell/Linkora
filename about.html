About Linkora

Built for one class,
not the whole internet

Linkora is a private, class-based web platform that helps students discover project ideas and form balanced teams using skills, interests, preferred roles and availability. Students with an idea publish their requirements, define the members they need and review suitable classmates. Everyone else explores projects, applies for roles or accepts invitations.

After a team is finalised, the platform provides a shared workspace for communication, task planning and progress tracking — while faculty keep a read-only view of team formation and progress.

Objective 1

Structured team formation instead of random grouping.

Objective 2

Skill-based matching with a transparent score.

Objective 3

Fair participation, with alerts for unassigned students.

Objective 4

Verified, class-level private community.

Frequently asked questions

Who can join my class workspace?
Only students who register with a verified college email, PRN number and the class invitation code issued by your coordinator.
How is the compatibility score calculated?
Required skills 50%, shared interests 20%, availability 20% and relevant experience 10%. Every recommendation shows the individual factors so you can see why a match was suggested.
Can I apply to more than one project?
Yes. You may apply to several projects, but you can only join one team per subject. Once you accept an invitation, your other pending applications are marked inactive.
What happens if my team is missing a skill?
Skill-gap detection highlights the missing role and the most valuable skill to add, then recommends classmates who have it.
Can faculty see our team chat?
No. Faculty see team composition, milestones and progress percentages only. Day-to-day chat stays private to accepted members.
What if two students post the same idea?
Duplicate-idea detection compares your title and problem statement with existing projects and warns you about overlaps, with an option to merge teams.
How are contributions recorded?
Every task in the workspace has an owner and a state. Contribution tracking counts assigned versus completed tasks for transparent evaluation.
Can I edit my project after publishing?
Yes. Open the project, use Edit, and update roles, skills, timeline or the deadline at any time before the team is finalised.

Privacy policy

Linkora collects only what is needed to run a class workspace: your name, college email, PRN number, class, skills, interests, availability and project activity. Your data is scoped to your class community and is never shown to students outside it or used for advertising.

Faculty receive aggregate progress information only. Reported content is reviewed by class coordinators. You may request deletion of your account and associated content at any time from the administration page.

✓ Class-level separation✓ Role-based access✓ No third-party tracking

Terms of use

Accounts are for verified students of the registered class. Posting spam, plagiarised project ideas or content unrelated to academic work is not permitted and may be removed by a coordinator.

Teams are formed transparently: accept only invitations you intend to honour, and credit every member's contribution honestly. Linkora records assigned and completed tasks to support fair evaluation.

✓ Verified accounts✓ Honest contribution✓ Report abuse

Write to your class coordinator or the Linkora support desk and we will get back to you within one working day.
